# session device management

Let a member see and revoke their own active sessions. The `sessions` table
already backs bearer-token auth; surface it in account settings as a list with
created/last-used and a coarse device or user-agent hint, plus "sign out" per
session and "sign out everywhere else". Distinct from the owner's kick (which
drops another user); this is self-service control over your own devices.
